Files starting with "_" are ignored — copy this template to a new file
(e.g. budget-2027-notes.md) in the show's folder to publish a post.

"type" is the small kicker above the title: Episode Notes, Guest Profile,
Behind the Scenes, or anything else. "date" (YYYY-MM-DD) controls ordering,
newest first. "image" is optional.

The body is plain text. Blank lines separate paragraphs.
